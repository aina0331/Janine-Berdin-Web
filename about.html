Patricia Janine Dusaran Berdin

Born January 28, 2002 · Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu

Everyone met Janine Berdin twice. First as a teenager in a noontime talent show, singing other people's songs better than most adults could. Then, years later, as a songwriter who'd rather be honest than polished. Both versions are her.

Timeline

  • 2010 — Auditions for Star Circle Quest, lands in the top 10 as the only Cebuana contestant, and signs with Star Magic.
  • 2017–2018 — Wins Tawag ng Tanghalan Season 2 as grand champion at age 15.
  • 2018 — Signs with TNT Records and releases her debut single "Biyaya."
  • 2019 — Records "Nasa Puso" for Kadenang Ginto; takes a brief break after a health diagnosis, then returns that November.
  • 2022 — "Mahika," her duet with Adie, hits No. 1 on the Billboard Philippines Songs chart.
  • 2024–present — Leaves Star Magic and signs with Island Records Philippines, embracing an indie, alternative sound and style.
